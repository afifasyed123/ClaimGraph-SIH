## High-Impact Presentation Talking Points
- **Automated Yet Legally Exact**: Notices are not vague email drafts; they cite the precise AICTE Approval Process Handbook (APH) clauses, exact numbers in dispute, and prescribed statutory remedies.
- **Tamper-Evident Anti-Corruption Security**: Every issued notice is sealed with a SHA-256 cryptographic hash and tied to an immutable audit block. Institutions, officers, or third parties cannot alter terms after issuance.
- **Public Trust & Verification**: The public verification endpoint and QR code allow institutions and state technical boards to verify notice authenticity instantly.
